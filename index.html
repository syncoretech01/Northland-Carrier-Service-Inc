Who we are

Northland Carrier Service Inc. specializes in the safe and reliable transportation of bulk materials. From ready-mix and precast concrete to hot-mix asphalt, sand, gravel, crushed stone, fill dirt, topsoil and construction debris — if it builds the site, we haul it. We are Other Specialty Trade Contractors who treat every load like it's the one holding up your schedule.

How it works

Five steps from call to compaction.

01

Request

Call dispatch or send the material, tonnage, site and window. We confirm availability within the hour.

02

Schedule

Routes are planned around plant hours, site access and weigh stations, with drivers assigned by material class.

03

Load

Every load is ticketed at the scale, tarped and photographed. Hot-mix is temperature-logged at the plant.

04

Deliver

GPS-tracked trucks arrive in sequence so crews never wait — and never get buried.

05

Confirm

Digital tickets, tonnage totals and site sign-offs land in your inbox the same day. Auditable and invoice-ready.
